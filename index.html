About this forecast
Datasets named "+ WRF (3 km)" are forecasts of the WRF model computed at the Czech University of Life Sciences Prague; the global model named in the dataset supplies their initial and boundary conditions. The other datasets show that global model's own 2 m temperature and precipitation, cropped and redrawn on this map. In both cases the source data were modified; nothing shown here is an official product of the providers below, and they do not endorse this service.
The time axis is the same for every dataset: five days from today's 00 UTC. The shaded part shows where the chosen dataset has a forecast, the darker part what is already loaded. "Run" in the header is the initial time of the model run on the map. ELEV in the value box is the terrain height of the model on screen, i.e. the 3 km WRF terrain or the coarser terrain of the global model. Your location, if you turn it on, is used only in your browser to mark the place on the map; it is not sent anywhere.
Click the map to pin a grid point, then "Compare models" in its box: the charts show 2 m temperature and precipitation at that place from every WRF run (solid lines) and every global model (dashed lines), each read at its own grid point.
