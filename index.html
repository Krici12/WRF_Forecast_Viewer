About this forecast
Datasets named "+ WRF (3 km)" are forecasts of the WRF model computed at the Czech University of Life Sciences Prague; the global model named in the dataset supplies their initial and boundary conditions. The other datasets show that global model's own 2 m temperature and precipitation, cropped and redrawn on this map. In both cases the source data were modified; nothing shown here is an official product of the providers below, and they do not endorse this service.
